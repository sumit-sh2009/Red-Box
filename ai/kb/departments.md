# Municipal department responsibilities (India-oriented, generic)

## Public Works Department (Roads)
Responsible for potholes, damaged carriageways, footpaths, street repair, and road cave-ins. Escalate if the defect sits on a school or hospital approach.

## Water Supply & Drainage
Leaking mains, dry taps, clogged storm drains, sewage overflow, and waterlogging after rain.

## Sanitation & Solid Waste
Missed garbage collection, overflowing bins, dumping in public land, and street sweeping failures.

## Electricity / Street Lighting
Dead street lights, sparking transformers, exposed cables on public land, outages affecting crossings.

## Public Safety & Disaster
Immediate hazards: collapse risk, fire, electrocution, accident-prone unrepaired pits.

## Education / Municipal Schools
Civic defects that block access to government schools or endanger students on the approach road.

## Transport & Traffic
Signals, bus stops, illegal parking that blocks a civic asset — coordinate with traffic police.

## Municipal Administration
Construction debris, noise, encroachments, and anything without a clear specialist owner.

## Escalation
High severity + school/hospital/water main → same-day inspection. Recurring cluster (3+ reports, same place) → treat as one work order, not three tickets.
